Profile

Zijing Yang 杨子靖

I am a senior undergraduate student in Electronic Information Engineering at Tianjin University. I am currently an exchange student at The University of Hong Kong, where I work as a Research Assistant in the CGVU Lab under the supervision of Prof. Taku Komura.

My current interests are in embodied intelligence, human-centered perception, and robotic systems. I am interested in how embodied systems can perceive people and their surroundings, reason about physical interactions, and act in the real world.

Before coming to HKU, I worked in the DEX-ROB Lab at Tianjin University, led by Prof. Shan An, on robotic dexterous grasping and visual perception systems. I also participated in international exchange and summer research programs, and enjoy working across the boundary between learning-based perception and real robotic systems.
